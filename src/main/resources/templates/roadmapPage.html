The roadmap follows a natural difficulty progression divided into 4 tiers: Level 1 (Foundations & Linear Structures), Level 2 (Non-Linear Structures & Problem Solving), Level 3 (Trees, Graphs & Dynamic Programming), and Level 4 (Advanced Data Structures & Master Algorithms). You can follow it linearly or focus on specific weak spots.

What happens when I click on a roadmap item?

Clicking any node in the list instantly opens the official LeetCode problem list for that topic (e.g. Dynamic Programming redirects to https://leetcode.com/problem-list/dynamic-programming) in a new tab so you can start solving right away.
