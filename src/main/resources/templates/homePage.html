Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LeetNode, problem difficulty ratings, and personalized recommendations.

Who created LeetNode and which coding community is it built for?

LeetNode was created by Sreecharan Kesireddy from IIIT Nagpur (IIITN) the lead of CP/DSA domain, GDG. It is engineered for students across IIITs, IITs, and the broader competitive programming (CP/DSA) community preparing for contest ratings and technical coding interviews.

What is LeetNode and how does it help my LeetCode prep?

LeetNode is a precision preparation platform for LeetCode. Instead of relying on broad and misleading Easy, Medium, or Hard labels, LeetNode calculates continuous contest difficulty ratings (e.g., 1650 or 2150), provides personalized practice problems tailored to your contest rating, and lets you filter problems by exact rating ranges.

How is a LeetCode problem's contest rating predicted?

LeetNode analyzes historical contest performance data, live contest solve percentages, user rating distributions, and algorithmic problem characteristics to calculate an Elo-style contest difficulty rating. This accurately reflects how difficult a problem proved to be for actual competitive programmers.

Why are continuous ratings better than Easy, Medium, and Hard?

On LeetCode, "Medium" problems can vary wildly from rating 1400 (approachable for beginners) to rating 2150 (requiring complex dynamic programming or advanced graphs). Practicing with granular ratings prevents hitting demotivating walls and ensures you train in your optimal growth zone.

How are personalized problem recommendations chosen?

When you enter your LeetCode username, LeetNode retrieves your contest rating and identifies your zone of proximal development—typically problems rated 50 to 150 points above your current rating. These problems are grouped by topic to systematically build algorithmic mastery.

Can I practice problems within a specific rating range?

Yes! With the Filter tool, you can specify any minimum and maximum rating (for example, 1600 to 1800 for Knight badge preparation or 2000+ for Guardian badge preparation) and get a paginated list of matching problems with direct links to solve them.
